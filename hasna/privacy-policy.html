Privacy Policy

App: HASNA حَسْنَاء: Prayer, Qur'an & Dua (Android package com.codeaxee.hasna)
Developer: codeAxee
Effective date: 11 November 2026  ·  Last updated: 12 November 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how the mobile app HASNA ("the App", "we", "us") handles information. It applies to the App on Google Play and any other store where it is distributed. By using the App you agree to this Policy. If you do not agree, please do not use the App.

In short:
  • HASNA is free and has no ads, no in-app purchases and no accounts.
  • There are no analytics or tracking tools. We do not use Firebase, advertising SDKs or crash-reporting SDKs.
  • Everything you enter stays on your phone. This includes your profile, prayer settings, location for prayer times, worship counts, bookmarks, notes, journal and the private diary.
  • We do not collect, sell or share your personal data.
  • The private diary and journal are encrypted on your device and are left out of device backups.
  • You can delete everything at any time inside the App, or by uninstalling it.

1. Information We Collect

We do not collect any personal information. The App has no sign-up and no login. It does not send your information to us or to anyone else. We do not have a server that receives your data.

2. Health and Religious Information

Some information you may enter is sensitive. Examples are the private diary (health related), your gender, and your worship records (which can reveal your religion). We never receive this information. It stays on your device, it is not used for advertising, and it is never shared. Diary information is not shown in notifications. You can also turn on App lock (your device fingerprint, face or PIN) and Discreet mode in Settings. The diary is educational and is not medical advice.

3. Internet Use and Downloads

The App works offline. It connects to the internet only when you choose to play or download recitation audio, download extra content, or open an outside link:

  • Qur'an recitations: when you press play, the audio is streamed from EveryAyah (unless you downloaded it before), and you can download whole sūrahs from the reciter screen. These requests contain only the file being fetched; no account, identifier or personal information is sent.
  • Content packs (when available in a later version) that you choose to download: extra ḥadīth books and translations, extra translations and tafsīr, history books, and offline mosque map data.
  • "Read more" links that open a book or page on an outside website in your browser.

The exact list of sources is shown in the App under More → About → Sources. When you open an outside website, that website's own privacy policy applies.

4. How We Use Information

Information you enter is used only on your device to make the App work for you: prayer times, reminders, progress, personalised content and your private notes. We do not use it for advertising, profiling or analytics, and we cannot see it.

5. Notifications and Alarms

Adhan alerts, prayer reminders, adhkar reminders and other reminders are scheduled on your phone. No server sends them and they reveal nothing to us. You can turn each one on or off in Settings.

6. Ratings and Reviews

After you have used the App for a few days, it may show Google Play's own review window so you can rate the App. This window is run by Google. We do not learn whether you rated the App or what you wrote, except what you choose to publish on Google Play. You can also rate the App or send feedback from More → About.

7. Information Shared with Third Parties

We do not sell, rent or share your personal information, because we do not collect it. There are no advertising, analytics or data-broker partners. The only third parties involved are:

  • Google Play, which delivers and updates the App and may show the review window (Section 6). Google's privacy policy: policies.google.com/privacy.
  • The download sources in Section 3, and only when you download something or open a link.

If you choose to share content from the App (for example an āyah image), it goes to the app you pick, under that app's policy.

8. Crash Reports

The App contains no crash-reporting SDK. If you have allowed your Android device to share usage and diagnostics with Google, Google Play may give us anonymous crash and performance statistics (Android vitals). These do not include your name, your entries or your location.

9. Device Backup

If Android backup is turned on for your Google account, Android may back up the App's ordinary settings (for example your prayer settings and bookmarks) to your Google account under Google's backup terms. The private diary and journal are always excluded from backup. Exports are created only when you choose, as a file that you save where you choose; an encrypted export option is provided.

10. How We Protect Your Information

  • Your data stays on your device and is protected by Android's app sandbox.
  • The private diary, journal and reflections are stored in an encrypted database; the key is protected by the Android Keystore.
  • Optional App lock with your device biometrics or PIN; sensitive screens can block screenshots and recent-apps previews.
  • All downloads use HTTPS and are checked (size and file type) before use.
  • The App writes no personal information to system logs.

No method of storage is completely secure, but keeping your data on your own device, encrypted where it is sensitive, removes the risks of a central server.

11. Data Retention and Deletion

Your data stays on your device until you delete it. In Settings → Data you can delete the diary, delete the journal, or delete all data at once. Uninstalling the App also removes all of its data from your device. We hold no copy, so there is nothing for us to delete on our side.

12. Children's Privacy

HASNA is for a general audience and is not directed to children under 13. The Kids' corner is designed for parents and guardians to use together with their children, behind a parent lock. The App does not collect personal information from anyone, including children. If you believe a child has sent us personal information (for example by email), contact us and we will delete it.

13. Your Rights (GDPR, UK GDPR, CCPA/CPRA and similar laws)

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, delete or move your personal data, to object to processing, and to complain to a data-protection authority. Because HASNA keeps your data only on your device, you can do all of this yourself inside the App (view, edit, export, delete). We do not sell or "share" personal information for cross-context behavioural advertising. If you email us, we use your email only to reply and delete it when the matter is closed; you can ask us to delete it at any time.
